What We Offer

Hotel Amenities

Facilities

Everything You Need

From world-class dining to reliable power and seamless connectivity — Skado is built to make your stay effortless and enjoyable.

Restaurant & Bar

Our in-house restaurant serves authentic Nigerian cuisine and continental dishes three times a day. The bar is stocked with premium local and international spirits, cocktails, and chilled drinks — perfect for unwinding after a long day.

24-Hour Power Supply

We understand that uninterrupted power is non-negotiable. Skado runs on a robust generator backup system that kicks in seamlessly, ensuring your AC, TV, WiFi, and phone chargers are always on — no matter what happens on the national grid.

High-Speed WiFi

Stay connected with our fast, reliable internet across all floors. Whether you're on a video call, uploading presentations, or streaming your favourite shows, our fibre-backed connection has you covered.

Free Parking

Arrive by car and park with peace of mind. Our secured on-site parking is available to all guests at no extra charge, with 24-hour security surveillance to keep your vehicle safe throughout your stay.

24-Hour Security

Your safety is our priority. Our professionally trained security team is on-site around the clock, with CCTV cameras installed throughout the premises and controlled access to all guest floors.

Laundry Service

Travelling light or simply need a quick refresh? Our laundry service handles washing, drying, and pressing with a fast turnaround. Drop off at the front desk and your clothes come back impeccably clean.

Conference & Event Space

Host meetings, trainings, seminars, or private celebrations in our fully equipped conference hall. With projector, PA system, air conditioning, and flexible seating arrangements for up to 80 guests.

Room Service

Enjoy meals, beverages, or snacks in the comfort of your room. Our room service is available during restaurant hours, with a curated in-room menu to satisfy any craving at any time of day.

Dedicated Concierge

Our front desk team is available 24/7 to assist with everything from local transport and restaurant recommendations to event planning and airport transfers. We go the extra mile so your stay is seamless.
