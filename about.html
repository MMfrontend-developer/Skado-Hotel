Our Story

About Skado

The Beginning

Built on Purpose,
Rooted in Nigeria

Skado Hotel and Suites was born from a simple but powerful vision: to create a space in Oyo State where guests are not merely customers, but honoured visitors. Founded by a family with deep roots in Oyo's business and hospitality community, Skado opened its doors with a commitment to setting a new standard for accommodation in South-West Nigeria.

From day one, we invested in what matters most to Nigerian travellers — reliable power, clean and modern rooms, genuine security, and warm human service. We didn't chase trends. We listened to guests, iterated, and built a reputation one stay at a time.

Today, Skado is a trusted name among corporate travellers, families on holiday, event organisers, and road-trippers passing through Oyo State. Our story is still being written — and every guest is a chapter in it.

Core Values

The Principles We Live By

01

Guest First, Always

Every decision we make — from hiring to room design — is measured against one question: does this serve our guests better? The answer shapes everything.

02

Reliability Without Exception

Power, water, security, WiFi — these aren't bonuses at Skado. They're guarantees. We invest heavily in infrastructure so you never have to worry.

03

Genuine Nigerian Warmth

Hospitality is cultural. Our team greets every guest not as a transaction but as a welcome visitor — with the warmth and care that makes Nigeria's people renowned worldwide.

04

Continuous Improvement

We actively seek feedback and use it. Skado today is better than Skado last year — and we will continue this discipline until we are unmatched in the region.

05

Integrity in All Things

Transparent pricing, honest communication, no hidden charges. What you see on this website is exactly what you get when you arrive.

06

Community Pride

We employ locally, source food from regional producers, and contribute to Oyo State's growth. Skado's success is tied to the prosperity of this community.
